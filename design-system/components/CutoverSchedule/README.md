CutoverSchedule sets how an upgrade runs as a schedule: each step a row with its bar on one shared, not-to-scale timeline, a hold diamond where someone signs, and your system's water line above, broken only at the step that names a planned shutdown.

Look `cutover-schedule` (`assets/src/blocks/look-cutover-schedule.js`) on a `steps` block with holds. Used on svc-upgrades, "How an upgrade runs". Concept adapted: services.1 (hold points as nodes on a line), set as a Gantt: downtime as a schedule.

- Markup: `div.aq-cutover-schedule` > `__lane` (`__lk`, `__ltrack` with `__water` segments and `__gap` (`__gl`)) + `ol.__rows` > `li.__row` (`is-cut`, `is-hold`) (`__n`, `__txt` (`h3.__t` with `a.__go`, `p.__d`, `p.__hold` with `__hk`), `__track[aria-hidden]` (`__grid`, `__bar` with `__diamond`)) + `p.__nts`.
- Fields: `h`, `items [{t, d, hold, link}]`, `ground`; new optional `lane` (the water line's drawing label). Every bar is the same length (the page gives no durations; "Not to scale" says so). The shutdown step is the first whose text names a shutdown; its words label the gap.
- Tokens: water `flow` 3 px; bars 1.5 px `ink` on `surface-raised`, the shutdown bar filled `ink`; hold stamp 1.5 px `ink` mono.
- Phones (pass 12, ~2,500 px from 2,950): no bar under each row. One mini schedule `div.__mini` (aria-hidden, phone-only) is a PhoneKit sticky strip (`data-pk-sticky`, `data-pk-h="96"`, the strip `data-pk-fig`): the step number in view at 44 px on the left (`span.__mn`), then the water line with its shutdown gap and all the bars stair-stepped (`span.__mb`), the bar of the row being read filled `flow` (`data-pk-key` / `data-pk-row`, `.is-pk-active`); the row's own number turns `flow` too. Rows are compact cards (number, name with its placeholder, line at 15 px, hold stamp); a linked name has a 44 px hit box. Desktop (the stair-step over the shared timeline) unchanged; motion's water line is scoped to the desktop lane.

Motion: time runs left to right: the water line runs to the shutdown, the gap opens with its label, the line runs on; each bar grows as the line passes, its row rising; the hold diamond and stamp strike. Once, about 3.5 s. Hover tints a bar. Phones (the lane hidden, pass 12 verify): each row rises on its own trigger as it comes into view, number, name, line and hold together. Under 860 px the hold sentence reads at 14 px sans after its mono HOLD key.
