CoverageMap sets a coverage table by region as one column per region beside an instrument readout: the region in hand, its severity 1 on-site time as the large figure, a seal stamp naming the CRN body and the engineering seal, and the tile map with its provinces lit.

Look `coverage-map` (`assets/src/blocks/look-coverage-map.js`); pass 9 built it as a held tile map beside region rows, pass 9b rebuilt it from concept `contact-locations.2` (region columns with a large readout in the top right cell) and kept the tile map inside the readout. The concept's "04 HR" was invented: the readout only shows what the cell says (today a placeholder).

- Data: a `table` block with `"look": "coverage-map"` (an `as: "router"` field may stay). `cols`: [region, then the columns that answer for it]; `rows` as the table; value cells may be `{t, link, intent}`. Plus `map`: `[{c, n, x, y, r}]`, one tile per province or territory: code, full name (the tile's title), grid column and row, and `r`, the index of the row that covers it. Optional `base: {c, label}`: the home tile and its label, copy taken from the page ("Based in Ontario"); its row is on the readout when the page opens.
- Layout: an `ol` (`role="list"`) of three cells to a row under a `line-strong` rule. Region column (`__col`): `line` left hairline (a 2 px `flow` one when in hand), the index in mono at top right, the region in Geist 300 32/38 as a button (`__pick`, `aria-pressed`, `aria-controls` the readout; underlined in `flow` when pressed), the tile codes as small mono chips, then each column as a mono label over its value on `line` hairlines (the first value in mono).
- Readout (`__now`, the third cell, top right; `limestone`, `data-theme="light"`, 3 px `line-strong` top rule, inner hairline frame; FxContour behind its lower half): one face per region (`__nv`, the one in hand shown) in an `aria-live="polite"` box: index and code, the region in Geist 300 28/32; the large figure (`__big`): the first column's cell up to " in " outside a placeholder, Geist Mono 300 72 px in `flow` (a placeholder keeps its chip at 18 px); the seal stamp (`__stamp`, a double ring): "CRN" and "Seal" over the bracketed acronym that follows each in the last column, or the body's name when it has none (no stamp when the cell names neither); then the first column's label with the rest of the cell ("in [Ontario centres served]") under it. The mono parts that decode carry `__sc`. Under the faces: the tile map (aria-hidden; tiles in mono 12 px, the region's tiles filled `flow` with `on-navy` text, the home tile with a 3 px `navy` edge and a square mark) and the base line.
- Behaviour (look file): pointing at a column, focusing or pressing its name, or pressing a tile puts that region on the readout; the choice persists. The wrapper hears `aqcoverage` ({r, instant}). Every column is always shown: the readout only repeats one.
- Every tile is a `button` (`aria-label` the province, `aria-pressed`, `data-p` the row it picks): a tile with a row of its own (`r`) picks that row; one without picks the row named like the tile (Quebec), else the row for the rest (the last row whose label starts "Other"). Tiles without a row keep their dashed edge; a picked one fills `flow-tint`. The desktop render is unchanged.
- Phones (pass 12, S11/S14; under 860 px): the tile map is the province picker. The readout sheet shows the map (44 px tiles, 2 px gaps, no inner hairline frame) and the base line; its face folds away (the stamp stays when the region has one), and only the chosen region's column shows under it (the base row's at first), so the region is never read twice. Placeholders in the column at 14 px. contact-locations.2: 3,204 -> 1,059 px at 375. Pass 12 verify: under the map a row of region chips (`button.__chip[data-p]`, the row names, 44 px, `aria-pressed`) does what a tile does, so no region hides behind the map; a tile that picks a named row (`[data-named]`) gets a solid edge on phones (desktop keeps the dashed edge); a polite status (`__say`, visually hidden) names the chosen region when the readout face is folded; tiles and chips control the list (`aria-controls`). Under 860 px the tile map is at most 26rem wide (about 56 px tiles at 768).
- Not for site addresses (SitePlates) or for real geography: the tiles are a schematic.

## Motion

Motion (`motion.js`): the region columns rise in one after another; the readout wipes in from the left, the region in hand decodes (text-scramble port: 20 steps of 40 ms, letters up to the progress shown as they are, random capitals and digits after them, on the face's mono parts only, so placeholder chips keep their markup), its stamp strikes (scale 1.12 to 1, 0.22 s), then the tiles are laid west to east and the base line follows. One timeline under 2.5 s, once on scroll-in. Choosing another region decodes its face again and strikes its stamp (visitor-driven, may repeat; instant for keyboard). Nothing under reduced motion.
