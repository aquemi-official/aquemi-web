RoomLimit sets what a site provides for a Purified Water package as the room the package goes in: an isometric cutaway of the room with the package on its base frame, a dash-and-dot battery-limit line on the floor round it, and every connection the site provides as a tie-in where its run crosses the line, numbered to the rows beside the drawing.

- On the site: a `spec` block with `"look": "room-limit"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-room-limit.js`), on first-system "What your site needs" (#site). Concept: pass 12 image 228, `design-system/inspiration/gemini/228-p12-first-system-site.png`. Not taken from it: its row words, the mono `[placeholder]` rows, the title block and the page title used as the heading; the rows are the block's own, and the two sides are named (the concept had no package/site split).
- Drawing (IsoKit, `assets/src/parts/IsoKit.js`): floor slab and two cut back walls; a door in the right wall with its width dimensioned on the floor (no figure); a power isolator on the wall with its conduit to the panel; a floor drain; the package (softener, carbon vessel, reverse osmosis rack, control panel on a base frame); city water through the left wall with its valve; the reject run to the drain; product water out to a point of use. The battery-limit line (`__bl`, `flow`, dash and dot) runs round the package; a diamond node (`__node`) sits where each run crosses it. Numbered navy squares (`__pin`, HTML over the drawing) sit on leaders from each row's point. The two sides are named on the floor (`__lab`, desktop only).
- Markup: `section.aq-room-limit-sec` → `div.aq-room-limit` (`__head`; `figure.aq-iso-figure.__fig` with `div.__pan` holding `svg.__svg`, the pins, the labels and the ring layer `__ringl > __ring`; `div.__rows` with the limit row `div.__item.is-limit`, `p.__side.is-pkg`, `ol.__list.is-pkg`, `p.__side.is-site`, `ol.__list.is-site`). An item: `span.__no` (the same navy square; `is-free` outlined when the row has no point), `h3.__p`, `p.__v`, `p.__m`, `p.__go > a.__link`. Column names (`cols[1]`, `cols[2]`) are read before the value and note by screen readers only (`__sr`).
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `cols`, `rows [{p, v, m, side, link, link_label, part}]` (side `limit` | `package` | `site`, default site), `sides [package label, site label]`. The same fields as BatteryLimit and ScopeSeam, so the three swap freely; `seam`, `fold` and `fold_noun` are not read (every row shows). `part` (look-only, DR-018 geometry): where the row sits on the drawing, one of `line`, `package`, `panel`, `water`, `drain`, `power`, `door`, `floor`, `use`; without it the row's words choose (limit row: the line; package rows: the package, or the panel for training; site rows by "drain", "power", "door"/"way in", "landlord"/"floor", "washer"/"bench"/"lab", "water"). Each place takes one row.
- Selection: pointing at or focusing a row (rows with a point are focusable) lights its square, leader and node, tints the row, and a `flow` ring rides a full-size layer to the row's point. Ported from Subframe Motion "Inside the image" (atlas-hotspots), MIT, (c) 2026 Subframe: 250 ms ease-out for the ring, 180 ms and 112 % for the square; instant under reduced motion. API: `window.rlPick(root, rowIndex | null)`.
- Desktop: title across the top, the drawing left (7/12, sticky under the header while the rows pass), the rows right (5/12) on hairlines; the limit row reads as the line itself (flow tint between two dash-and-dot rules). About 1,310 px at 1280 with nine rows.
- **Phones** (≤ 860 px): the drawing cropped to the line and its tie-ins (the walls' empty tops cut away; the crop is computed by the renderer from the line, the points and the numbers, `--rl-ar`, `--rl-w`, `--rl-x`, `--rl-y`), every number kept; the floor labels drop. Then the limit row full width with its link as a 44 px row, then each side as a two-column checklist: navy square and name on one line, value and note under it, all at 14 px or more. About 1,280 px at 375 (BatteryLimit had 2,000). No PhoneKit behaviour: every row is open and the checklist is short; links use the 44 px hit box.
- Tokens: `flow` line, nodes, ring and the package label; `navy` / `on-navy` squares; `flow-tint` for the limit row and the lit row; `line`, `line-strong`, `ink`, `ink-muted`; IsoKit's line classes for the drawing.
- Use it for site requirements around a package that sits in one room. For a split between what a package fits and what it does not, ScopeSeam; for a long list of site items with no drawing, BatteryLimit.

## Motion

On scroll-in the package draws itself (IsoKit choreography), the battery-limit line fades in and its dashes creep once round the package, the nodes press onto it, then each number strikes with its row in order (desktop) or the checklist rises as it scrolls in (phones). Once, under 5 s; nothing under reduced motion (the finished drawing shows).
