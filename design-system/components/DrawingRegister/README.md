DrawingRegister sets a short list of places water goes as a drawing register on a limestone sheet: a row of framed outline drawings with mono sheet codes, one steel-blue viewfinder over the selected drawing, and under the row that item's drawing at large with its notes and a title block.

Look `drawing-register` (`assets/src/blocks/look-drawing-register.js`) on a `grid` block (as "route"). Used on ind-hospital-lab, "Where water goes in reprocessing". Concept: pass 12, 209-p12-ind-hospital-lab-reprocessing (drawing register, one viewfinder, title block); the washer-disinfector is drawn as a cabinet unit (glazed door, rack shelves, control strip, plinth), not the concept's domestic washer; the concept's scribbled notes and filler part were not taken.

- Markup: `<div class="aq-drawing-register" style="--n;--sel">` > `__strip` (`ol.__cells` > `li.__item[data-i]` > `button.__cell[aria-pressed]` (`__frame` > `svg.__svg`, `__code`, `__name`), `p.__d` visually hidden; `span.__vf` aria-hidden) and `__detail` (aria-hidden: one `__pane[data-i]` per item: `__zoom` with `__dim` lines and `svg.__svg--big`, `__notes` (`__nl`, `__nt`, `__nd`, `p.__tb` title block)).
- Drawings: outline front-and-side views drawn by the look, one per kind (`draw` field): washer, ultrasonic, sink, endoscope (with its own feed filter), cart, sterilizer (water feed valve only), else a plain cabinet. Water lines in `flow`. No dialysis equipment and no steam generator is ever drawn. Sheet codes are the kind's initials (WD, UC, MS, ER, CW, SF): drawing labels, not model numbers.
- Fields: `h`, `nav` (the title block's sheet name), `items [{t, d, draw, link}]`; without `draw` the kind is read from the name.
- Behaviour: pointing at a drawing (mouse), focusing it or tapping it selects it: the viewfinder glides there (240 ms, .2/.85/.25/1, from Subframe Motion "Project index", MIT) and the detail shows that item. Works with motion off (the glide is a CSS transition; instant under reduced motion). Buttons carry `aria-pressed`; every item's name and line are in the page for readers.
- Tokens: section `aq-band--limestone` forced light; frames `surface` on `on-limestone` lines; viewfinder `flow` at 26 % with a 2 px `flow` edge; notes 32 px light; title block mono on `on-limestone` rules.
- Motion (`motion.js`): frames rise and their drawings ink on left to right, the viewfinder settles over the first drawing, the detail's drawing inks on as the notes rise. Once, under 3.5 s.
- Phone (860 px and narrower): drawings two to a row (frames about 165 px, name 14 px, each a 44 px+ tap target); the selected frame is outlined and tinted in steel blue; under the grid the viewfinder block on `flow`: the drawing in `on-navy` lines, Notes, the name (24 px), the line (15 px) and a three-cell title block. No moving overlay.
- Tablet (561 to 860 px): drawings three to a row, and in the steel-blue block the large drawing sits beside its notes (5:7), about 920 px tall at 768. Notes eyebrow in `on-navy` (contrast on `flow`).
- Not for: lists longer than six (the row gets cramped), or items without a physical thing to draw (use a list look).
