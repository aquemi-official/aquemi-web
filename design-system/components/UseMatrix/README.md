UseMatrix shows an industry-by-use table as one water main: a twin-wall pipe runs along the column heads and tees down into every use column, and each cell is a square on its column's line, navy where a grade is set and open where the cell is a dash.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"look": "use-matrix"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-use-matrix.js`); industries.1 "Industry by water use". Origin: pass 12 concept `design-system/inspiration/gemini/194-p12-industries-matrix-take2.png` (idea only: its rows, columns and fully filled grid are not ours; the blank pattern is the content's).
- Fields: `cols` [row label, use 1 … use n] (2 to 6 uses), `rows` ([industry string or `{t, link}`, cell …]), `intro`. A cell `"—"`, `"-"` or empty is a blank. A short bracketed source in a cell (24 characters or fewer: `(EMA)`, `(ST108:2023)`) is set as a small mono tag `span.__src`; longer brackets stay as text.
- Markup: `div.aq-use-matrix` (`--c` uses) → `div.__heads` (aria-hidden: `p.__h0`, `p.__h[data-c]` each with `span.__drop` > `span.__water`), `ol.__key` (phones), `ol.__list` (PhoneKit accordion) of `li.__row[data-pk-item]`: `button.__tap[data-pk-head]` (name, `span.__strip` of `span.__ms` numbered squares, chevron), `h3.__name[data-pk-desk]` (the link), `div.__cells[data-pk-body]` (display: contents on desktop) of `div.__cell.is-set|is-blank[data-c]` (`span.__sq`, `p.__v` with the column name in `span.__k`, visually hidden on desktop) and the phone row link `a.aq-phone-kit__go.__go`.
- The pipe: each head cell draws its stretch of the trunk (`::before`, two `line-strong` hairlines over the ground with a 2 px `flow` line) across the column gap to the next; the last stops at its drop; the first has a double-rule inlet. Drops start at the trunk's water line. Pointing at a cell or head lights its column (`is-col`: navy squares turn `flow`, the drop's walls `flow`).
- Tokens: `navy` filled squares, `line-strong` open squares on `surface`, `ink` set text, `ink-muted` blanks and heads, mono 11 source tags. Rows 18/24 names, 14/20 cells.
- **Phones** (≤ 860 px, PhoneKit accordion, first row open): the uses named once in a key (numbered outlined squares, two columns, mono 12); a twin-wall riser down the left with one tap row per industry branching off it (16/22 name, the row in miniature as five numbered squares, navy where set, chevron; heads 48 px+). The open row lists every use as a chip (navy for set, outlined for blank) with its 14/21 text, then the industry page as a 48 px row link. Without script every row shows open with the same chips. Height at 375: 1,167 px (the table-to-card stack it replaced was 2,861).
- Not for tables whose cells are long paragraphs or for more than six uses; use a plain table or ReaderSplit.

## Motion

Ported from Subframe Motion "Animated workflow diagram" (process-choreography), MIT: each column's drop fills with water top down in turn over one 1.12 s run, and as it lands its filled squares stamp in down the column (scale from 0.4, 60 ms apart). Phones: the tap rows step in once. Reduced motion: the finished table.
