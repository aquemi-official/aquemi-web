RevisionRegister keeps technical notes as the numbered notes column of one limestone drawing sheet: the title large and light with the lead note under it, a faint drawing of the page a note is applied on, and the register in mono behind a hairline rule, with a topic filter and a title block carrying the live count.

- On the site: News, "The notes register" (`register` block, `"look": "revision-register"`; renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-revision-register.js`, which also carries its filter and the drawing selection). Origin: pass 9b concept `research/pass9b/concepts/news.0.png` (limestone drawing sheet: big title left, notes as the sheet's numbered notes column in mono, faint schematic between); first built in pass 9 from `63-concept-notes-register.png`.
- Markup: `section.aq-band--limestone` → `div.aq-revision-register[data-theme=light]` (navy hairline frame) → `div.__grid` → `div.__left` (the section head, `aside.__lead`: `p.__lk` triangle, status and number, `blockquote.__q` the note's `d`, `h3.__lt`, `p.__lstd`, `p.__go` "Applied on" link, or "Read the note" when the link points at the register's own page), `div.__fig` (aria-hidden: `span.__frame` with registration marks, one `span.__dw[data-k]` Spot per page a note is applied on, `span.__figcap`), `div.__col` (`div.__strip` → `div.__tabs[role=group]` of `button.__tab[aria-pressed][data-topic]` with counts; the fold with `ul.__notes` of `li.__note[data-topic][data-k][data-i]` → `span.__no` "01." (+ triangle on the lead), `div.__nb` → `h3.__nt` title link, `p.__meta` status · topic · standard); then `div.__tb` title block (`p.__count[aria-live=polite]`).
- Fields (a `register` block): `h`, `intro`, `topics`, `items [{t, topic, std, date, link, featured, spot, d}]`, `fold`, `fold_noun`. Rules kept from NotesRegister: never a date that is not given, the lead note is `featured`, else the first of the first topic; a tab shows every matching note, folded or not. Counts count what exists (DR-018 rule 6): a dated note counts as a note; an undated one, or one whose `date` is still a placeholder (opens with "["), counts as a topic. A topic's status is its placeholder date, or else the name of the page that answers it (a dashed mono status); as the lead it is "Featured", never "Latest". No "planned" or "next" wording (DR-028). So the title block and the live count read "25 topics" while no note has a date ("3 notes · 22 topics" once some have), and the "All" tab reads "All 25 topics". A note's drawing: its `spot`, else the page's (sys-monitoring TOC, sys-pw RO, sys-storage tank, sys-pretreatment softener), else the page icon.
- Tokens: `limestone` sheet with `on-limestone` / `on-limestone-muted` text, `navy` frame, column rule, title block and pressed tab, `flow` only for links, the triangle and the quotation rule; the drawing in `navy` at 55 %. Geist Light 64 title; Geist Mono for numbers, titles in the column, status, topics and standards.
- Container under 1100 px: no drawing, title and lead beside the column; phones: title, lead note, filter, notes, title block across the foot.

## Motion

Motion: the frame rules round the sheet, the column's rule drops, the tabs drop in, the lead's triangle strikes and its quotation rises line by line; each visible note is written in order (number decodes, line rules in left to right); the drawing prints from the bottom and the title block appears. Once, under 3.5 s. Filtering: notes that stay glide to their new places (280 ms, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)) and new ones fade in (180 ms), ported from Subframe Motion "Filter mosaic" (filter-mosaic); instant under reduced motion. Pointing at or focusing a note shows its page's drawing (240 ms crossfade, the selection persists), ported from "Hover image preview list" (project-index).
