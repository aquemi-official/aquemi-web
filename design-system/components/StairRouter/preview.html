Where to start

Every project runs in four stages: design; build and test; install and hand over; operate. Find where your project stands below.

  1. 01
    01 / 08

    New system

    Your situation
    New system, user requirement specification (URS) approved or in draft
    Start with
    Process design: request a URS gap review
    What to send
    Your URS, feed water analysis, peak demand in L/h
    What comes back
    A compliance matrix marking every gap, within [URS review turnaround, working days]
  2. 02
    02 / 08

    Not under GMP

    Your situation
    Not under GMP: a hospital, lab or process specification
    Start with
    Process design: request a specification review
    What to send
    The standard and its edition, or the equipment maker's specification; feed water analysis
    What comes back
    A compliance matrix against it, within [URS review turnaround, working days]
  3. 03
    03 / 08

    Design approved

    Start with
    Fabrication: request a spec review
    What to send
    Approved drawings, piping and material specification, province
    What comes back
    Clauses we meet or would change, a draft inspection plan, within [document review turnaround, working days]
  4. 04
    04 / 08

    Skid in build

    Your situation
    Skid in build, factory and site acceptance tests (FAT, SAT) ahead
    Start with
    FAT and SAT: request a specimen FAT protocol
    What to send
    Your work email
    What comes back
    Test cases with acceptance criteria, within [specimen package response time, working days]
  5. 05
    05 / 08

    Installed

    Your situation
    Installed or arriving, qualification or acceptance ahead
    Start with
    Commissioning and qualification (C&Q): request a plan review
    What to send
    URS or specification, validation master plan if any, points-of-use list
    What comes back
    Draft C&Q and sampling plans, within [document review turnaround, working days]
  6. 06
    06 / 08

    In service

    Your situation
    In service, qualified or accepted
    Start with
    Service: request a plan quote
    What to send
    System, age, instruments, standby and ride-through time
    What comes back
    Plan, annual price, first-visit tasks, within [quote response time, working days]
  7. 07
    07 / 08

    Aging

    Your situation
    Aging, out of support, or about to change
    Start with
    Upgrades: request a system assessment
    What to send
    Drawings, a year of water data, the problem
    What comes back
    Scope and price within [assessment response time, working days], then options with downtime and requalification or a repeat acceptance test
  8. 08
    08 / 08

    Specifying for a client

    Your situation
    Consultant or contractor
    Start with
    Writing your URS: notes for specifiers
    What to send
    Your client's URS or specification, and the bid date
    What comes back
    A compliance matrix against it, within [compliance matrix response time, working days]
