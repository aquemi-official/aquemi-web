When polypropylene is the wrong choice

Our package is polypropylene (PP) with polyvinyl chloride (PVC) pretreatment. If your URS needs any row below, our package does not fit as written. We will tell you early.

    If your URS needsA loop held hot, routine 80 °C sanitization at loop pressure, or steam sterilization
