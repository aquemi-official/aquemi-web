TrainStrip sets one treatment train as a P&ID strip on a navy band: PVC pretreatment, duplex RO in parallel, the tank, loop pump and UV, heat exchanger, endotoxin filter and an isolatable PP loop with its points of use, sensors and sanitization port, a numbered marker under each station and a numbered caption per stage below.

Look `train-strip` (`assets/src/blocks/look-train-strip.js`) on a `diagram` block. Used on ind-hospital-lab, "One train, designed to the critical-water limits". Concept: pass 12, 211-p12-ind-hospital-lab-train (navy P&ID strip, duplex RO, valved loop, numbered captions). Valves are drawn as PP diaphragm valves (bow-tie body and domed bonnet, no flanges); no dialysis or steam equipment.

- Markup: `<div class="aq-train-strip" style="--n">` > `__strip` > `svg.__svg` (pipes `__pvc` and `__pp`, `g.__node[data-ts]` per station, `g.__mark[data-ts]` with `__lead`, `__mk`, `__mkn`, flow `__chev`, material `__tag`s) and `ol.__stages` > `li.__stage[data-ts]` (`__glyph` svg for phones, `__no`, `h3.__t`, `p.__d`).
- Stations: gen (two PVC housings, two RO trains each behind a valve), tank, pump (pump and UV), hx, pou (endotoxin filter housing, isolation valves either side of three taps), sensors (CE bubble and a sample valve), spray (capped sanitization port). The strip is fixed; the only text in it is the P&ID tags PVC, PP and CE.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `stages [{t, d, node}]` (`items` also read). `node` picks the station; without it stages map in order gen, tank, pump, hx, pou, sensors, spray.
- Tokens: `aq-band--navy`; PVC pipe `on-navy-muted` 6 px at 55 %, PP pipe `limestone` 3 px, equipment `navy` fill with `on-navy` lines, tank level `flow` dashed, markers `navy` with `limestone` edge; hover `flow`.
- Behaviour: pointing at or focusing a caption or a station lights the other (flow stroke, marker filled, caption rule).
- Motion (`motion.js`): the pipes draw left to right in one pass, each station arrives as the water reaches it (Subframe Motion "Process choreography" pacing, MIT), markers drop in, captions rise. Once, under 4.5 s.
- Phone (860 px and narrower): no strip; a riser: a limestone pipe down the left with each stage's P&ID symbol on it in a 40 px navy box (RO, tank, pump, exchanger, diaphragm valve, CE bubble, capped port), one row per stage (number mono, name 17 px, line 14 px). Every stage shown, no fold.
- 861 to 1100 px: the strip keeps its desktop drawing with larger SVG labels (19 units, about 11 to 13 px rendered; markers r 18) and the captions in rows of four. SVG labels are 14 units at wider widths (about 12 px at 1280). Phone heading 34/40 px.
- Not for: a storage and distribution loop explained station by station with its return riser (LoopCircuit), or a pretreatment train alone (SectionTrain).
