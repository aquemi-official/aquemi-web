FitReadout shows a fit test read off an instrument: a navy readout plots the loop material's pressure rating falling as it heats up to one limit line, beside the test's numbered rule tiles, with the rows that are not served or answered elsewhere as outlined tags that link their homes.

- On the site: a `spec` block with `"look": "fit-readout"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-fit-readout.js`); industries.3 "Where the package fits, in any industry". Origin: pass 12 concept `design-system/inspiration/gemini/193-p12-industries-fit.png` (idea only; its axis figures and tags are never copied).
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `caption` (the readout's title strip), `rows [{p, v, m, link, link_label, hl}]`, `tiles` (how many rows are tiles, default 3). The readout reads the `hl` row (else the first): its axis words are its condition split at "and" ("Temperature and pressure"), and axis numbers appear only when its answer states a range such as "0 to 80 °C" (the lower end at the origin, the upper on the limit line); otherwise the axes are bare. The chart is aria-hidden: every word in it is in the tile beside it.
- Markup: `div.aq-fit-readout` → `figure.__readout` (`p.__cap`, `svg.__chart`: `__out` hatched field past the limit, `__grid`, `__ax`, `__curve`, `__tail`, `__lim`, `__dot`, `__end`, `text.__lab`, `text.__val`), `ol.__tiles` of `li.__tile` (`.aq-index`, `h3.__t`, `p.__v`, `p.__m`, `a.__go`), `ul.__tags` of `li.__tag` (`.is-not` when the answer starts "Not": dashed outline, the answer as a mono stamp; `p.__tp`, `p.__tv`, `p.__tm`, `a.__go--tag`).
- Desktop: readout 5/12 (sticky under the header while the tiles pass; it sits in `div.__col`, a column box stretched to the tiles' row, so the hold ends with the tiles and never slides over the tags, pass 12 round C), tiles 7/12 stacked, tags in one row under both (auto-fit, 15rem minimum).
- Tokens: `navy` readout with `on-navy` curve, `on-navy-muted` axes and labels, `on-navy-muted` limit line and `on-navy` end point and light (theme-stable, so the limit reads in dark too); tiles on `surface-raised` with `line-strong` edges, numbers 40 px mono light (`flow` on the readout's row); tags `line-strong` outlines. Links are 44 px targets.
- **Phones** (≤ 860 px): the readout first at full width (axis labels about 13 px rendered, values 14 px), then the tiles stacked with the number inline before the title (28 px), answer 15/22, basis 14/21, then the tags stacked full width. At 600 px and under (pass 12 round C) the tiles become a ruled list (no card, 12 px rows, numerals kept; the tags' top rule closes it) and the tags become one ruled list with no card padding: each row is its title with the stamp beside it, then its basis or its link, secondary lines 14 px. Height at 375: about 1,506 px (1,768 before; the rule-figures render it replaced was 3,418).
- Not for a table of many conditions with no single governing curve; use RuleFigures or a spec table.

## Motion

Entry: the curve draws from the cold end to the limit line (1.1 s), the limit line drops in and the hatched field fades up, then the tiles and tags step in. Pointing (fine pointers only), ported from Subframe Motion "Chart crosshair tooltip" (chart-crosshair), MIT: the reading point snaps to the curve under the pointer's x and eases back to rest on leave. Reduced motion: the finished readout.
