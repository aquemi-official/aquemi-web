What a Purified Water URS should settle

Number each requirement, one sentence each; suppliers answer in your numbering. Hospitals and labs issue a basis of design; industrial users send the equipment maker's spec. The same order helps.

  1. 01

    Before design

    Feed water, grade, demand and site limits first. Then the loop: scheme, components, check against URS and budget, change or freeze.

    Start
  2. 02

    Water grade and system boundary

    Purified Water to USP, Ph. Eur. or both, by edition, or your standard's grade. Where the system starts and ends; what is by others.

    Water grade and system boundary
    Grade
  3. 03

    Demand and points of use

    Every point of use, washers and humidifiers too: peak draw in L/h, daily volume, temperature and planned growth.

    Demand
  4. 04

    Feed water

    Feed water analyses from every season, and the coldest feed temperature: cold feed cuts membrane output.

    Feed
  5. 05

    Quality limits and where you prove them

    Name each limit and its pharmacopoeia, where it is measured (online or lab), and who sets alert and action levels.

    Limits
  6. 06

    Sanitization, then materials

    Settle the sanitization method first: it decides the materials. Then write materials as performance, unless a standard names one.

    Sanitization, then materials
    Sanitize
  7. 07

    Controls, alarms and data

    Where failed water goes, restart after power loss, user access, audit trail, data retention and backup, screen language.

    Controls
  8. 08

    Room, utilities and approvals

    Room size and access, power, air, drains, room temperature, and who obtains each Canadian approval, such as pressure registration.

    Site
  9. 09

    Testing and acceptance

    Who writes and approves each protocol, and whether factory results count toward qualification. Acceptance: tests, numeric criteria, run length, signatures.

    Tests
  10. 10

    Documents, service and spares

    A dated document schedule, handover set, response hours, spares on site, training hours, warranty start event.

    Handover
