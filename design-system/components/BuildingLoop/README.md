BuildingLoop sets the places a central plant feeds in one building as a building section on a navy band: one pale loop leaves the plant on the ground floor, threads three floors to numbered stops, and returns under the slab; each stop is named in the margin beside it, with a numbered caption per stop under the band.

Look `building-loop` (`assets/src/blocks/look-building-loop.js`) on a `grid` block (as "route"). Used on ind-hospital-lab, "Where water goes in a laboratory building". Concept: pass 12, 210-p12-ind-hospital-lab-labs (navy band, three-storey section, one loop, labelled stops; the concept's six stops were its own invention, the look draws one stop per item).

- Markup: `<div class="aq-building-loop" style="--n">` > `__fig` (`__margin--l`, `__draw` > `svg.__svg`, `__margin--r`; margins aria-hidden, each `__label[data-bl][style=--y]` = name + `[0N]`) and `ol.__caps` > `li.__cap[data-bl]` (`__no`, `h3.__t`, `p.__d`). In the svg: building hairlines (`__wall`, `__slab`, `__hair`, `__win`, `__gr`), `__loop`, `__glint`, plant, and one `g.__stop[data-bl]` per item (fixture `__fx`/`__fl`, `__drop`, `__lead`, `__tick`, `__ring`, `__num`).
- Stops: up to six, in flow order on fixed positions (top floor left then right, middle floor right then left, ground floor left then right; five items skip ground-left). A stop on the left half is named in the left margin, else the right.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `items [{t, d, draw}]`. `draw`: the fixture drawn at the stop (washer, analyzer, bench, humidifier, hood); without it the kind is read from the name, else a bench tap. The drawing carries no copy beyond the items' own names and numbers.
- Tokens: `aq-band--navy`; building in `on-navy-muted` hairlines, fixtures `navy` fill with `on-navy` lines, loop `limestone` 3.5 px, rings `navy` with a `limestone` edge, mono numbers; hover `flow`.
- Behaviour: pointing at or focusing a caption or margin name lights its stop (ring, leader, fixture) and caption.
- Motion (`motion.js`): the building is ruled in, the loop draws from the plant in one stroke, stops and names land as it passes, then one glint runs the loop (Subframe Motion "Animated beam", MIT). Captions rise. Once, under 5 s.
- Phone (860 px and narrower): the section drawing at full width (343 px at 375; ring numbers about 12.6 px, margins and leaders hidden), then a riser: a limestone line down the left with each stop's numbered ring on it and one row per stop (name 17 px, line 14 px). Every stop shown, no fold.
- 861 to 1100 px: rings r 20 with 20-unit numerals (about 11.7 px at 900). Above 1100 px caption titles take a 44 px minimum height, centred, so every caption body starts level.
- Not for: a single treatment train (TrainStrip, LoopCircuit), or service visits round a plant (VisitPlan).
