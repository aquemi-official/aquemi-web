GhostSkid shows a system's configuration options where they would go on the base skid: the base Purified Water skid drawn solid in IsoKit line art on the limestone band, each option a dashed "ghost" module clipped on at its place, numbered to a ruled list of the options.

## Motion

The base skid draws itself through `AQiso.timeline` (parts back to front, pipes, the water runs it once); then each option clips on in number order: its ghost settles 10 px down onto the skid while its dashes run along their lines, and its leader and tag drop in; the rows rise in a stagger. Once, under 5 s. The running dashes are ported from Subframe Motion "SVG line drawing animation" (signature-draw), MIT, (c) 2026 Subframe (a `stroke-dashoffset` tween; here on the dash pattern), and repeat while a visitor points at an option. Reduced motion: the finished drawing, no running dashes.
