ReceivingBench sets supplier control as a receiving bench: an isometric bench with the supplies the block lists, each tagged with its row number, and a magnifier pair that joins the pipe's lot marking to the certificate's lot line.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"look": "receiving-bench"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-receiving-bench.js`), cmp-quality "What we require from suppliers". Origin: pass 12 concept 242 take 2 (`242-p12-cmp-quality-suppliers-take2.png`: five tagged supplies on a bench, a [lot] circle joined to a certificate circle). Replaces MarkingMatch on that page; MarkingMatch stays in the library. Not copied from the concept: the vessel on legs (drawn as a plain FRP vessel on its skirt), the loose second certificate circle, the "[lot]" text and rows without supply names (the rows carry the page's own words).
- Drawing (IsoKit primitives, composed in the renderer): a bench (furniture legs, a sunken apron, the top), then back to front 03 a PVC pipe length with a solvent-weld socket, 04 a membrane element (seal bands, end cap with spokes), 01 a PP pipe length with its bore, its printed marking and the lot group in `flow`, 02 two flat gaskets, 05 a small plain FRP pressure vessel (skirt, domed shell, one port on the top head and one at the foot; composed from `isoB` because IsoKit's `filter` part carries bed lines, a multiport head and a gauge that read as a media filter). Tag 05 sits on the open bench top in front of the vessel, clear of the membrane element. Each supply is an `aq-iso__part` with `data-iso-id` = its row number; tags are navy boxes with mono numbers on 1 px leaders. The pair: circle A (the pipe wall, the marking, the lot boxed in `flow-tint`), a `flow` join with a match tick, circle B (the certificate sheet, its lot line boxed, a seal). Labels "Lot marking" and "Certificate" are the page's own words. Candidate kit parts: the bench, the pipe length, the gasket, the membrane element and a plain pressure vessel.
- Markup: `<div class="aq-receiving-bench">` > `__fig` (`svg.aq-iso.__svg`: `g.__world` > parts, `circle.__spot`, `g.__tags` > `path.__tl`, `circle.__td`, `g.__tag`; `g.__mag` > `path.__ml` leader, `g.__lens` x2, `g.__join`, `text.__lab`; and `svg.__pair`, the phone pair) + `__sched` (`__facts` > `p.__fact` > `__fk`, `__fv`; `__head` aria-hidden column names; (Fold) `ol.__list` > `li.__row[data-rb-key]` > `h3.__name` (`__n` tag, name), `__c` x2 (`__k` label, `p` / `p.__how` with the `__ok` tick)).
- Fields: `facts [{k, v}]`, `cols [supply, what we require, how we verify it]`, `rows` (up to five drawn supplies in the order above), `intro`, `fold`, `fold_noun` (phones only).
- Tokens: `line-strong` line work on `surface` fills (IsoKit), tags `navy` with `on-navy` mono 12 px; lens rims `ink`, glass `surface-raised`, lot boxes `flow-tint` with a `flow` edge, join and tick `flow`; rows: name 17 px `ink`, require 15 px `ink-muted`, verify 15 px `ink` after a `flow` tick; header rule `navy`.
- Hover / focus: pointing at a row (or a supply, its tag or the pair) lights that supply (`is-lit`), its tag and the row's tag in `flow`, the row on `flow-tint`, and moves a `flow` spotlight ring onto the supply.
- Layout: figure (10 fr, sticky under the section bar) beside the schedule (11 fr). 861 to 1100 px: equal halves, the two cells stack under each name.
- Phone (860 px container or less): no bench; the two circles side by side with their join and labels (at most 420 px wide), then the rule, then the rows (tag, name, the column names as 12 px mono labels, 15 px text), fold after `fold` rows (2 on cmp-quality). No PhoneKit: the pair is the one idea and it belongs to row 01; the tags stay in the rows.
- Motion (`motion.js`): the bench draws through IsoKit's `AQiso.timeline` (1.4 s), the tags drop in, the leader draws from the pipe, circle A opens on the marking, the join draws to circle B and the tick lands; rows rise 8 px with their ticks drawn. The spotlight is ported from Subframe Motion "Product tour spotlight" (guided-inspection), MIT: it glides to the pointed supply over 550 ms cubic-bezier(.22,.75,.22,1). Reduced motion: the finished drawing; the spotlight jumps.
- Not for: documents or tests without a physical object to inspect (RecordTrail, NcrTiles).
- Tablet (600 to 860 px container): the bench drawing stays (max 560 px, centred) over the tagged rows, so the tags still point at their supplies; the phone pair is not shown. Below 600 px: the pair, then the rows.
- Dark: the navy tag chips (drawing and rows) carry a 1 px `on-navy-muted` hairline so the chip shape reads on the near-black band.
