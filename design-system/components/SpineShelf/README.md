SpineShelf shows a document package as the object itself: a row of binder spines standing on one shelf (one per section, each as thick as the section holds documents, the key section navy) beside the package index, whose sections open to list their documents.

- On the site: a `docs` block with `"look": "spine-shelf"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-spine-shelf.js`), ind-hospital-lab "For consultants and contractors" (pass 12, concept 212-p12-ind-hospital-lab-specification: navy key spine, numbered rail on phones; its all-caps spine titles were not taken: titles stay sentence case). Earlier on cmp-documentation "The turnover package, section by section" (section library specimen). Origin: concept 51-concept-turnover-binder (the package as a filed object on a navy-framed sheet), recast as spines and an index that opens, because compliance already files its standards in manila folders (ProofFolders).
- Markup: `<div class="aq-spine-shelf">` with `__shelf` (aria-hidden: `__row` of `__spine[data-i]` > `__body` > `__no`, `__holder` > `__name`, `__ring`; `__board`; `__cap`) and `__index` > `ol.__list` > `li.__item[data-i]` > `details` > `summary.__sum` (`__n`, `h3.__t`, `__lead`, `__mark`) + `ul.__docs`.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `items [{t, d, key, docs}]`, `fold_noun` (the shelf caption's noun). `docs` (optional) lists the section's entries; without it they are read from `d`, split at ";" or ". ", else at commas outside brackets (first letter capitalised for display). The first `key` item is the navy spine and opens first.
- Behaviour: opening a section (the summary is the keyboard control) pulls its spine up off the shelf with a flow edge; pointing at a row or a spine lifts its pair; clicking a spine opens its section. Works without motion; without script the details still open.
- Tokens: spines `limestone` in three shades (mixed with `on-limestone` / `on-navy`), key spine `navy`; index on `line` hairlines, titles 300 weight; `flow` only for the open spine's edge, open numbers and the key section's document marks.
- No fold (pass 12, S7): every section is listed at every width; closed rows are one line each. A `fold` field on the block is ignored.
- Phone (860 px and narrower, pass 12): no shelf. The index becomes a numbered rail: a hairline down the left with each section's number in a 32 px square on it; the key section comes first as a navy row (open, its documents listed in `on-navy-muted`), then the other sections as outlined rows (`surface-raised` on `line-strong`, 52 px tap rows, title 18 px, documents 14 px when opened). Rows sit above the rail line (z-index 1), so the line never crosses the key row's number. Desktop unchanged.
- Motion (`motion.js`): the shelf board draws, spines drop onto it left to right with a short settle, index rows wipe in, then the key spine is pulled as its documents deal in. Once, under 3 s.
- Not for: a short list of documents (use DocList or SheetPreviews), or evidence mapped to standards (ProofFolders).
