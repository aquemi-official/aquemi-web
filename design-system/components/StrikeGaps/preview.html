Gaps in older controls

PLC-5, SLC 500, S5 and S7-300 are discontinued or in phase-out: spares are limited.

GapWhat it risksWhat the upgrade does
  1. 01

    Gap: Shared operator login, or none

    What it risks: No record of who changed a set point or cleared an alarm

    What the upgrade does: Individual operator accounts with access levels

  2. 02

    Gap: A chart recorder as the release record

    What it risks: Paper charts that fade and are slow to review

    What the upgrade does: Time-stamped historian, or a defined paper record

  3. 03

    Gap: Backups never restored

    What it risks: A failed controller that cannot be rebuilt

    What the upgrade does: Restore test at OQ, repeated on a schedule

  4. 04

    Gap: Records only in the old system

    What it risks: Past water records lost at changeover

    What the upgrade does: Verified migration, or old records kept readable
