Which water each product class needs

Each product's legal class sets the minimum, in each market you sell to. One plant can make all four. Your specification may go higher, never lower.

Your product→What sets it=Minimum water

  1. 01

    Cosmetic only: creams, lotions, shampoos, makeup → What sets it: Health Canada encourages good manufacturing practice (GMP) and endorses ISO 22716 =

    Minimum water: Your written specification, or your brand client's

  2. 02

    Natural health product lines → What sets it: Health Canada GUI-0158 =

    Minimum water: Potable water at minimum; PW where your specification or a monograph names it

  3. 03

    Sold in the US as OTC drug-cosmetics: sunscreens, antiperspirants, fluoride toothpaste, hand sanitizers → What sets it: US drug GMP, 21 CFR 210 and 211 =

    Minimum water: Purified Water (PW), under drug GMP

  4. 04

    Drugs with a Canadian drug identification number (DIN) → What sets it: Health Canada GUI-0001 =

    Minimum water: PW, under drug GMP
