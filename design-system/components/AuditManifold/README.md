AuditManifold sets an evidence table (who reads, what they read, where we answer it) on a navy blueprint sheet whose column joins are pipe headers, so each reader's row reads as one route from the auditor to our answer.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"as": "evidence"` and `"look": "audit-manifold"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-audit-manifold.js`); used on ind-meddevice ("Who audits your water system"). Origin: pass 9b concept ind-meddevice.3 (auditor table on a navy blueprint grid, double-line manifolds with valves feeding each row across the columns).
- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy.aq-audit-manifold-sec` → `svg.__grid` (blueprint grid, aria-hidden) → `.aq-in` → section head → `div.aq-audit-manifold` → `p.__colh` (column heads, aria-hidden: the phone shows them as `span.__k` keys per cell) and the fold (`wFold`) holding `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` (`div.__who` with `span.__no` and `h3.__t`; `span.__pipe--a`; `div.__read` with `p.__v` in Geist Mono; `span.__pipe--b`; `div.__go` with `a.__link`). Each `span.__pipe` (aria-hidden) is a twin-wall header `span.__hdr`, a branch `span.__br`, a PP diaphragm valve `svg.__valve` (bow-tie body, domed `__bonnet`, the `__stem` position indicator the states turn) and an end cap `span.__fl` (no flange plates; the header ends are capped too); `i.__w` is the water line in each.
- Fields, as the evidence table: `h`, `intro`, `cols` [who, read, answer], `rows` [who, read, {t, link, intent} or text], `fold`, `fold_noun`. Every link, intent and the fold are kept.
- Tokens: `navy` ground, `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted` text and pipe walls, water `flow` mixed toward `on-navy`; Geist Light 22 for the auditor, Geist Mono 15 for what they read. The header starts at the first row's centre and ends at the last row's centre; a folded table's header runs on toward the "Show all" button.
- Rows: pointing at or focusing a row (its link) marks it `is-on`: its valves fill with water and their stems turn a quarter (open), its branch water brightens, the other rows step back to 62 % opacity.
- Never folds (pass 12, S7): the header pipes reference every row, so `fold` / `fold_noun` stay in the data and the look shows every row at every width (ind-meddevice: all five auditors under one continuous header with five take-offs).
- Access tables (pass 12): when any "what they read" cell says how the item is reached (on this page, on request, at audit, NDA), `ol.__rows` gets `is-access` and each row `is-open` (now / on this page), `is-half` (on request, at audit) or `is-closed` (NDA, with an aria-hidden `span.__nda` tag), read from the row's own words outside placeholders (a row whose access is still a `[placeholder]` gets no state and draws a dashed, undecided valve). Only the phone draws the states: open is a solid water valve, half a water outline, closed dry with the tag.
- Phones (≤ 860 px, pass 12): one header pipe runs down a 48 px left rail through every row (from the first take-off to the last); each row takes off it through one valve (open: water in the body and the stem turned a quarter; half: stem at 45° and the body half filled; closed: dry, with the "NDA" tag under it). The second pipe and the column keys go (no "Route"/"NOTE" labels); each row is the title (19 px), one 14 px line, and the answer as a full-width 48 px row link with its arrow at the right. cmp-quality 1,764 → about 1,290 px; ind-meddevice shows all 5 rows (765 px folded → 1,048 px). Desktop (1280) unchanged on cmp-quality.
- Not for: a table without a reader → document → answer route; use a plain evidence table or ProofFolders.

## Motion

Ported from Subframe Motion "Animated workflow diagram" (process-choreography): one progress value drives overlapping segments. The first header fills down row by row, each branch fills as the header passes it and its "what they read" brightens, then the second header and its branches, and each answer arrives as its branch reaches it (opacity, 6 px rise, 0.96 → 1 scale, cubic ease-out). One run on scroll-in (2.4 s), then the inline styles are cleared. Hover or focus on a row re-runs its two branches (0.18 s each) while the valves open in CSS.
