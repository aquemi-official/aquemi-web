RequestMenu sets a table of what each request returns as a short menu on a limestone sheet: the title oversized across the top right, an isometric skid drawn at the left, and one line per returned item with its When and Cost.

Look `request-menu` (`assets/src/blocks/look-request-menu.js`), pass 9b; built from concept `research/pass9b/concepts/contact-proposal.2.png` (cropped oversized headline, isometric skid line drawing at the left, a four-column situation / returns / when / cost menu with the time in mono and the cost in the accent). Replaces the pass 9 look `return-slips` (ReturnSlips, removed). Used on contact-proposal ("What each request returns").

- Data: a `table` block with `"look": "request-menu"`. `cols`: [situation, what comes back, when, cost]: the column heads (a clock icon on When) and, on phones, the field keys. `rows`: [situation, returns, when, cost]. A returns cell holding several items joined by " · " gives one line per item; the when and cost cells are split the same way when they hold the same number of parts, otherwise the whole cell spans the group's lines (rowspan). A `{t, link, intent}` cell keeps its link (and intent). `fold` and `fold_noun` fold whole situations as everywhere.
- Markup: `section.aq-band--limestone.aq-request-menu-sec` → `div.aq-request-menu-wrap[data-theme=light]` (light tokens on the limestone ground in both themes) → `div.__mast` (the section head) and `div.aq-request-menu` → `figure.__fig` (aria-hidden IsoKit drawing `svg.__svg`: skid base frame, open frame of four posts and top rails as single lines, tank, two filter housings in series, a closed module) and `div.__body` → `table.__table`: `thead` heads (`th.__h[data-col]`), then one `tbody.__set` per situation: its first `tr.__line` opens with `th.__s[scope=rowgroup][rowspan]` (`span.__no` mono index, `span.__st` the situation), then `td.__got`, `td.__when`, `td.__cost` (each value in `span.__v`; `span.__k` field keys, aria-hidden, shown on phones only).
- Type and colour: title Geist 300 at clamp(48px, 8.2cqi, 124px), from the third of the width to the right edge; situation Geist 300 22/28; items 16/24; When in Geist Mono 14; Cost in `flow`; rules `line-strong`, dashed between the lines of one situation. The section clips horizontal overflow (`overflow-x: clip`), so the sticky drawing still works.
- Columns: situation 28 %, returns 30 %, when and cost 21 % each; drawing 7 / menu 17 of the width.
- Phones (pass 12, S11/S12/S18): the title at 40 to 56 px; no drawing and no table. The rows render a second time as `div.__phone` (hidden on desktop): a key naming the columns once ("What comes back", a When chip with the clock, a Cost chip), then `ol.__acc`, a PhoneKit accordion of the situations (56 px heads: number, situation, chevron; first open). Each body lists the returned items at 15 px, each with its When chip (mono, clock) and Cost chip (`flow`) under it; screen readers hear the column name before each chip. The accordion follows the page's form: ticking a situation in "Where are you starting from?" opens the same situation here (a `change` listener in the renderer matches the radio value to `data-pk-item`). 3,054 to 1,346 px at 375. The desktop concept is still to come. The table heads stay for screen readers only.
- Not for a matrix the reader compares across (CompareTable) or for routes the reader picks (Router).

## Motion

`motion.js`: ported from Subframe Motion "Scroll-driven image reveal" (scroll-window): one scroll progress (sheet top from the viewport foot to 30 % from the top) mapped to a phase with clamp(p × 1.24), eased out, carries the title in from beyond the right edge (26 % of its width; 18 % on phones), so it starts cropped like the concept and lands whole before the menu is read. The page's one scroll-linked story; the words never fade. The skid draws with `AQiso.timeline` (1.3 s lines, then the water runs the route once); the menu's cells follow in reading order (12 px, 0.5 s, 35 ms apart). Ported from Subframe Motion "Chart crosshair tooltip" (chart-crosshair): pointing at or focusing a line snaps a `flow-tint` band under it (no easing), lights its situation's number and the column head under the pointer (a `flow` bar under the head). Nothing under reduced motion except the crosshair, which the visitor drives.

Pass 12: the form-to-accordion sync opens a situation only if it is not already open (PhoneKit open() toggles an open row shut).
