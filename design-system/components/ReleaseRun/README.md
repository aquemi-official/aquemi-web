ReleaseRun sets ordered steps as Swiss columns under one water line: a huge light numeral per step, a node where each step starts, and the last step as the navy column where the water is released.

- On the site: a `steps` block with `"look": "release-run"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-release-run.js`); used on ind-cdmo ("From signed program to released water"). Origin: no concept of its own (quota); adapted from services.1 (nodes on one line to a boxed result) and sys-pw.2 (Swiss columns, huge numerals).
- Markup: `div.aq-release-run` → `span.__line` (twin hairline, `span.__water`), `ol.__cols` of `li.__col` (`.is-end` navy) → `span.__node`, `span.__n` (Geist ExtraLight 168), `h3.__t`, `p.__d`.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `items` [{t, d, link}]. Placeholders in the words stay Placeholder chips.
- Tokens: numerals `ink` (the end column `limestone` on `navy`), water `flow`, nodes `ink` outline; hover a column: node and numeral `flow`. Heading Geist Light 56/60.
- Phones: the line turns upright down the left, steps stack beside it, the last still navy. Pass 12 (S13): each step's numeral at 48 px inline to the left of its name, the words full width under both, step names that link carry a 44 px hit box (first-system 1,580 to 1,140 px; buying 1,355 to 990 px).

## Motion

The line is laid, then the water runs along it; as it reaches each step the node seats, the numeral rises and the words follow; the navy column fills in under the arriving water. Once on scroll-in, under 4 s; nothing under reduced motion. Phones (pass 12 verify): the line and water draw down the rail and each step rises on its own trigger as it comes into view (no step waits blank in view); the navy end card is navy from the start.
