ApplicationWheel shows where one Purified Water package goes as an application wheel: the package in the hub inside its loop ring, one numbered segment per industry group round it, and each group's block (name and page link, limit tag, plant uses) beside its segment on a hairline leader.

- On the site: a `grid` block with `"look": "application-wheel"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-application-wheel.js`); industries.0 "Where our water goes". Origin: pass 12 concept `design-system/inspiration/gemini/191-p12-industries-map.png` (idea only; no copy, numbers or labels taken from it).
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `items [{t, tag, link, d}]`. `d` is split into plant uses at commas and semicolons (final period dropped, first letter capitalised); a `d` with any piece over six words is a sentence, not a list, and stays whole. Best for six groups (four to eight draw).
- Markup: `div.aq-application-wheel` (`--n`, `--rows`, `data-aw-r0/r1`) → `svg.__leads` (leader layer, filled by script), `div.__wheel` (aria-hidden `svg.__svg`, viewBox 400: `__disc`, `g.__seg[data-i][data-wp]` annular sectors, `text.__num`, `__hub`, `__loop`, the IsoKit package `svg.__pkg`) and `ol.__list` (display: contents) of `li.__grp--r` / `--l` (`--row`): `p.__n`, `h3.__t` with `a.__link`, `p.__tag`, `ul.__uses` (`span.__ui` numbers like 01.2).
- Desktop: three columns (blocks, wheel `--wheel` = min(42cqw, 580px), blocks); the first half of the groups runs down the right column, the rest up the left, so segment i (centred at -90 + (i + 0.5) x 360/n degrees, clockwise from the top) faces its block. Leaders are measured, not guessed: the look's script draws, per block, a hairline from the middle of its inner edge, level, then angled into its segment's rim with a dot (redrawn on resize through a ResizeObserver; without script there are no leaders and the blocks still read).
- Pointing at a block or a segment lights both and the leader (`is-hot`; segment `flow-tint`, leader and dot `flow`). Segments carry `data-wp` so a click opens the group's page; the keyboard path is the block's link.
- Tokens: `surface` disc, alternate segments mixed from `line` (60% into `surface`, so they turn with the theme), `line-strong` hairlines, `flow` loop ring (dashed) and lit state, `ink-muted` numbers. Type: names 20/26 sans, tags 12 mono outlined, uses 14/20 sans with 11 mono numbers.
- **Phones** (≤ 860 px container): the wheel on top at up to 320 px (segment numbers 22 units, about 18 px rendered; the package in the hub), a twin-wall riser with water down the middle from the wheel's foot, and the groups as a two-column grid branching off it (a stub to each block's top rule). Names are 44 px link targets, uses 14 px, use numbers hidden, leader layer hidden. Height at 375: 1,535 px.
- Not for more than eight groups or for groups without pages; use IndustryFeed for two to four destinations of the same water.

## Motion

Ported from Subframe Motion "Animated workflow diagram" (process-choreography), MIT: one 1.12 s trunk-then-branches run with a cubic ease-out. The hub's loop ring turns in first, the segments open out of the hub clockwise in turn, each leader draws as its segment lands and its block arrives as the leader reaches it.
