ContactRoutes deals the ways into Aquemi onto a navy band as route sheets, each showing what happens after you act as a numbered sequence on a water rail that ends at its response clock, so a buyer on a contact page sees which route fits and what follows before the form.

Origin: `inspiration/gemini/62-concept-contact-routes.png` (pass 9): the route line joining folder-tabbed sheets, the 1 → 2 → 3 strip under each, the urgent route as the dark sheet with its call bar. The concept's in-sheet upload form and map are left to the EnquiryForm and CoverageMap sections that follow.

- Data: block `"type": "contact-routes"` with `routes: [{t, d, steps: [3 strings], clock, link, phone?, urgent?, current?, action?, intent?}]`. `link` is a page id or `page#anchor`. `clock` is the full response line ("Written reply within [proposal response time, working days]"); every time stays the page's own placeholder until Aquemi confirms it. `action` overrides the button label (default: the linked page's nav label); `intent` ticks options on that page's form, as on Button.
- Band: the section is `aq-band--navy aq-contact-routes-sec`. A dashed route line (`on-navy-muted` mixed into `navy`) runs under the heading and drops into each sheet's folder tab.
- Sheets: one column per route on one subgrid (rows: tab, head, call bar, steps, clock, action; `__body` is `display: contents`), so the step rails, clocks and actions line up across sheets. Ordinary routes are `limestone` sheets with `data-theme="light"`; the urgent route is the `navy` sheet with a `line-strong` edge and `data-theme="dark"` (its title in `flow`), so the tokens inside each sheet keep contrast in both page themes.
- Tab (`__tab`): "Route N" in data order, then "· Urgent" and/or "· This page", mono 12/16 uppercase, cut on a slant at its right like a folder tab.
- Body (`__body`): the title in Geist 300 at 30/36 and one line in `ink-muted`; the urgent call bar (`__call`: phone icon, "Call", the number in mono 300 at 36/44, a `tel:` link once real); then the steps (`__steps`): a `line-strong` rail with the water (`flow`) over it, one node per step (the first filled), "01" in mono and the step in 17/24. The rail runs on to the foot of the body, into the terminus.
- Terminus (`__clock`): the rail ends in a square clock mark; "Response" eyebrow and the clock line in Geist 300 at 20/28.
- Action (`__action`): the current route (`current: true`, or `link` equal to the page being read) gets a primary button that jumps to this page's form (the form block's `nav` id; without one, the first form) and a flow top edge; the others a secondary button to their page. Buttons are full width and 44 px or taller.
- Phones (`@container (max-width: 860px)`, pass 12, visual audit S11: about 2,000 px down to about 1,000): sheets stack in markup order, urgent first, on a riser down the left with a stub into each tab. Each sheet reads head (title 22/28, line 14/20), then the response line (clock mark 24 px, the "Response" eyebrow dropped, the line 16/24), then a "How it works" toggle (`button.__how`, 44 px, chevron, `aria-expanded` / `aria-controls`; closed by default) that opens the numbered steps under it, then the action. The urgent route's number is a full-width call button in the action fill (56 px; once the number is real the `tel:` link covers the whole box; until then the placeholder shows as text) and its own action turns outlined, so on contact-service, where the urgent route is this page, the call is primary and "Log a service request ↓" secondary. The route for this page (not urgent) is one line: its tab, then its title as a 56 px anchor (`a.__jump`, phone only) down to this page's form; its body is not shown. The toggle works with motion off (handler in `blocks/contactroutes.js`); desktop never shows the toggle or the jump row and always shows the steps. Tab numbers follow the phone's own order (`__rn-p`: Route 1 is the urgent route at the top; desktop keeps the data order in `__rn-d`). The urgent route is tighter: call box 56 px with the phone icon alone (no "Call" word), its response line straight under it with no rule. The urgent tab (both widths) sits on the urgent sheet's `navy` with a `line-strong` edge, its marks in `ink` (4.5:1 or more in both themes).
- Markup (`blocks/contactroutes.js`): `ul.aq-contact-routes` (`--routes`) of `li.aq-contact-routes__route` (`is-current`, `is-urgent`, `--o`, `data-theme`): `p.__tab` (`__rn`, `__mark`), `div.__body` (`div.__main` with `h3.__t`, `p.__d`; `p.__call` with icon, `__k`, `__num`; `button.__how` with `__chev`; `ol.__steps` (id) of `li` with `__node`, `__n`, `__s`), `p.__clock` (`__end` with icon, `__k`, `__v`), `div.__action`, and on this page's route `a.__jump`.
- Keep it to three routes and three short steps each. Not for a list of related pages (RelatedLinks) or for choosing a technical route by situation (Router).

## Motion

Motion (`motion.js`): the route line draws across under the heading; at each sheet its drop runs into the tab, the sheet rises, the tab lifts out of its top edge and the head settles (the urgent call bar presses in); then the water runs down the rail (`--aq-line`), each node pops as it arrives with its step, and the clock mark strikes at the end of the rail. Desktop: one timeline in data order (under 4.4 s); phones: each sheet as it scrolls in, urgent first. Hover or focus on a sheet lifts its tab and fills its nodes. The variables (`--aq-riser`, `--aq-stub`, `--aq-line`) default to 1, so the drawing is complete without motion.

Pass 9b (Subframe Motion `animated-beam`): a glint runs a sheet's step rail to its response clock, popping each node, once to close the entrance (this page's route, else the urgent one) and again when the pointer or focus enters a sheet; the call number presses.

Phones (pass 12): each sheet still enters as it scrolls in; opening "How it works" runs the beam down the opened rail once (`AQrouteBeam`, skipped under reduced motion).
