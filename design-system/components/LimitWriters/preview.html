Same package, different limit-writer

Someone writes your water limit: a pharmacopoeia, ANSI/AAMI ST108:2023, ASTM D1193-24 or CLSI GP40, your recipe or your equipment maker. The industry decides which; the water train stays the same.

What stays the same: the stages and their materials.

  1. 01

    Feed water

    Your city or well water. Its analysis sizes the pretreatment, whatever you make.

  2. 02

    Pretreatment

    A polyvinyl chloride (PVC) train removes chlorine, hardness and particles before reverse osmosis (RO). The same in every industry.

  3. 03

    Generation

    RO, then deionization (DI) or electrodeionization (EDI) where your grade needs it. The outlet is designed to meet your limit-writer’s number.

  4. 04

    Storage

    Fitted where your use needs stored water: [configurations offered for this use].

What changes: sanitization, monitoring and documents.

  1. 05

    Distribution loop

    Changes by industry: how often it is sanitized, and whether an endotoxin limit applies.

  2. 06

    Points of use

    Changes by industry: what you sample and the records you file, at the product, rinse, washer, bench or electrolyser.
