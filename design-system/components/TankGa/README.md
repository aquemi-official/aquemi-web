TankGa reads a spec block of fabrication parameters off one general arrangement (GA) sheet: an isometric line drawing of the PP storage set with a numbered tag on a leader for each parameter, the parameters as a ruled key beside it, and the budget in the title block.

- On the site: a `spec` block with `"look": "tank-ga"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-tank-ga.js`); used on sys-storage ("Tank and pipework", anchor `tank`). Concept: pass 12 #230 (`design-system/inspiration/gemini/230-p12-sys-storage-tank.png`, a tank and pipework GA sheet with numbered leaders keyed to ruled rows). Not copied from it: its flanged exchanger and metal-looking spools (every run here is plain PP pipe with socket-fused couplings, no flanges), its repeated tag numbers (one tag per row, from the content), the budget in every row (once, in the title block).
- Drawing (IsoKit, `aq-iso--raised`): the `tank` part on legs with a vent filter housing on its head; hidden lines inside for the water line, the dip pipe and the spray ball with its jets; the outlet to the `pump` with a drain `valve` on the suction; the discharge rising through a double tube sheet exchanger spool (shell, two rings at each end, two service stubs) and out to the loop; the return falling back over the set to the spray ball nozzle; a dimension on the tank's straight side. Purified Water storage only: no stainless, no still, no flanged metal.
- Markup: `div.aq-tank-ga` (PhoneKit root: `data-pk-sticky data-pk-h="220" data-pk-zoom="1"`) → `div.__head` (the section head: caption as eyebrow, h2), `div.__fig` (aria-hidden, `data-pk-fig`) → `div.__pan` (`data-pk-pan`: `svg.aq-iso` with `g.__lead[data-part]` leaders, `span.__tag` per row with `data-pk-key`), `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` (`tabindex=0`, `data-pk-row`, `data-part`: `span.__n`, `h3.__p`, `p.__v`, `p.__m`), `div.__block` (title block: `span.__brand`, `span.__cap`, both aria-hidden, and `div.__budget` with `h3.__bl`, `p.__bv`, `p.__bm` holding the basis and the row's link).
- Fields: `h`, `caption`, `rows` [{p, v, m, link, link_label, part}]. `part` places a row's tag: `volume` (the dimension), `contact` (the shell), `surface` (the inside wall at the water line), `joint` (a fused coupling), `design` (the head), `flush` (the drain valve), `hx` (the exchanger), `pump`, `spray` (the spray ball), or `budget` (the row goes to the title block; the first such row only). Rows without a known part are tagged to the shell. Tags are numbered in row order, skipping the budget row. `fold` is ignored: every tagged row shows (S7).
- Tokens: sheet `surface-raised` in a `line-strong` frame on the section's ground; tags `navy` with `on-navy` 12 px mono numbers, the chosen one `flow`; key numbers in a `navy` outline; leaders `line-strong`, the chosen one `flow`; a chosen part's lines `flow` over `flow-tint`; key rows ruled in `line`; the budget cell `navy` with `on-navy` text and `on-navy-muted` basis.
- Interaction (in the look, works without motion): pointing at, focusing or clicking a key row selects it (persists): its tag fills and grows to 1.12, its leader and part turn flow, the row takes `flow-tint`, the other rows quiet to 0.62. Instant under reduced motion. The selection logic is ported from Subframe Motion "Image hotspots" (atlas-hotspots), MIT, (c) 2026 Subframe.
- Mid widths (up to 1100 px): drawing above, key two rows a line, title block under. Phones: see below.
- When not to use it: parameters that name no part of a storage set (use a plain spec table or another spec look); a docs block (AuditSkid pins documents to a skid).

## Motion

`motion.js`: the IsoKit choreography (`AQiso.timeline`) lays equipment, pipes and the water run once; the hidden inside of the tank fades in with the pipes; then each leader draws from its part to its tag, the tag lands (scale 0.6 to 1, power3.out) and its key row rises 8 px, 0.09 s apart. Once on scroll-in, about 3.5 s. Phones: the rows rise at once as the parts start, 0.04 s apart, so the key never sits empty. Reduced motion: nothing runs, the sheet is complete.

## Phones (pass 12)

- Uses PhoneKit's sticky compact figure: the drawing is a 220 px strip held under the header, the whole set fitted to its height (no pan), the active tag and part lit; the row on the reading line is selected as on desktop (tag filled, part in flow, a 2 px flow rule on the row; the other rows are never quieted). Tags keep their on-screen size against the zoom.
- The key is compact: tag box, parameter at 16 px, value and basis at 14 px (placeholders at the 13 px chip size). Every row shows.
- The title block closes the sheet: the brand on one mono line (the caption cell is dropped: it repeats the eyebrow), the budget as a full-width navy card with its link as a 44 px target.
- Under 600 px the sheet gives its width back (S14): no side frame, the paper runs edge to edge and the text keeps the 16 px gutter.
- Without script the phone shows the drawing full width above the key.
