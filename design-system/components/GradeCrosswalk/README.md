GradeCrosswalk sets a grades table that maps water types to two families of standards as a crosswalk: the water types run down the middle as navy blocks, one side's standards stand as chips on the left and the other side's on the right, and hairline wires join every standard to every water that cites it.

Look `grade-crosswalk` (`assets/src/blocks/look-grade-crosswalk.js`) on a four-column `table` block. Used on ind-hospital-lab, "Which water each standard asks for" (hospital standards left, laboratory standards right). Concept: pass 12, 208-p12-ind-hospital-lab-grades (navy water-type blocks, clause chips on hairline wires, editions in mono; the concept's unlinked skid drawing, repeated codes and leaked prompt text were not taken).

- Markup: `<div class="aq-grade-crosswalk">` > three `__head`s (`--a`, `--m`, `--b`), `__rail--a` > `ol.__nodes` > `li.__node[data-gcn][data-rows][style=--p]` (`__std` name, `__ed` edition with a visually hidden `__sep`), `svg.__wires--a` (`path.__wire[data-gc][data-gcn]`), `ol.__rows` > `li.__row[data-gc]` > `__block` (index, `h3.__t`, `p.__how`, `p.__note`) + `ul.__refs` (`li.__ref` > `__side`, `__cite`, `__rnote`), then `svg.__wires--b` and `__rail--b`. Rails, wires and heads are aria-hidden; each row's `__refs` carries its citations as text.
- Geometry without measuring: blocks share equal heights, so block i's centre is (i + 0.5) / N; each standard's chip sits at the mean of the rows it serves (`--p`), pushed apart to at least 0.55 of a row; wires are a stretched svg with non-scaling strokes.
- Fields: `cols [water, how, standards A, standards B]`, `rows [[water, how, citesA, citesB]]`. Cites split on ";"; "ANSI/AAMI ST108:2023" shows as name "ANSI/AAMI ST108" and edition "2023" (the separator stays in the text for readers); a segment starting in lower case ("values from D1193-06") is a note on the citation before it, shown in the block in mono, never a chip. An empty cell means no standard on that side: no wire.
- Tokens: blocks `navy` with `on-navy` names (24 px light) and `on-navy-muted` text, chips `surface-raised` on `line-strong`, editions mono `ink-muted`, wires `line-strong`; hover `flow` (wires, chip fill, a flow edge on the block).
- Behaviour: pointing at a block lights its wires and chips; pointing at a chip lights every block it serves.
- Motion (`motion.js`): blocks land down the middle one after another; each one's wires run out to its standards on both sides and a chip ticks in the first time a wire reaches it. Once, under 4 s. Phones: blocks rise with their citation rows.
- Phone (860 px and narrower): no rails, wires or heads. One navy block per water (index and name on one line, how it differs under it, 14 px), then its citation rows on hairlines under the block: the side ("Hospital standard") once per side, the standard name 15 px with its edition in a mono box, the note in mono. Every row shown. Between the name and the edition, a 12 px mono "also 02 · 06" names the other blocks that cite the same standard (from the rows, no new facts), so the crosswalk idea survives without wires.
- Not for: requirement-to-clause mappings with many clauses per row (ClauseCrosswalk), or a ladder of grades (GradeLadder).
