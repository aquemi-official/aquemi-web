Who writes your limit, and what to send

Outside drugs and natural health products, no regulator sets a Purified Water grade for these uses. Your recipe, your process or your equipment maker sets the limit.

  1. 01RecipeFood and beverage

    Recipe or brand specification

    What it sets

    Ingredient water above the potable legal floor: hardness, alkalinity, chlorine, minerals

    Where it appliesFood and beverage

    What to send

    • Your water profile
    • Feed water analysis
  2. 02CannabisCannabis products

    Cannabis quality assurance person

    What it sets

    Potable water at minimum (Cannabis Regulations, s. 88.6), plus the methods they approve

    Where it appliesCannabis products

    What to send

    • Their water specification
    • Any pharmaceutical standard your site runs
  3. 03ProcessIndustrial and energy

    Process engineer

    What it sets

    Conductivity in µS/cm at each rinse or process stage

    Where it appliesIndustrial and energy

    What to send

    • The limit, its stage and unit
    • Flow at each stage
  4. 04ElectrolyserIndustrial and energy

    Electrolyser stack maker

    What it sets

    Feed-water limits for the stack

    Where it appliesIndustrial and energy

    What to send

    • The stack maker's feed-water specification, with revision
  5. 05BoilerIndustrial and energy

    Boiler maker or treatment program

    What it sets

    Make-up water quality for the boiler

    Where it appliesIndustrial and energy

    What to send

    • The make-up specification
    • Your treatment program's targets
  6. 06ASTM D1193-24Hospitals and laboratories

    ASTM D1193-24 Type II or III

    What it sets

    Laboratory reagent water, by type

    Where it appliesHospitals and laboratories

    What to send

    • The type and edition your method or customer names
