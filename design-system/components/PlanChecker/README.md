PlanChecker compares service plans as a checker of square tiles on limestone: one column per plan alternating navy and limestone, a head tile with a huge light slash numeral, the plan name and who it suits, a terms tile whose rows line up across the plans, and each plan's own button.

Look `plan-checker` (`assets/src/blocks/look-plan-checker.js`) on a `table` block with `as: "plans"`. Used on svc-service, "Service plans: response, scope, price". Concept: research/pass9b/concepts/svc-service.0.png (plan columns as alternating navy/limestone tiles, huge slash numerals, price and response tiles below).

- Markup: `section.aq-band--limestone.aq-plan-checker-sec[data-theme=light]` > `div.aq-plan-checker[data-snap-box][style=--n,--rows]` > `__tabs` (phones: `button.__tab[data-snap-to][aria-current]` with `__tabno`) + `ol.__plans > li.__plan.t<N>` (`is-base` for "No plan", `is-rec`) > `div.__head` (`p.__no` with `__num` and `__sl`, `__mark`, `h3.__name`, `p.__suits` with `__sk`), one `div.__term[data-r]` per term (`p.__v` value, `p.__k` label; `is-first`), `div.__act` (the button). Tiles carry `is-navy` or `is-lime`.
- Fields: as PlanTiers. `cols` [label, plan ...] (a head may be `{t, intent}`), `rows`; the row whose label starts "Suits" goes in the head tile; `recommend` (only when the page says so); `fold`, `fold_noun` (terms shown first). The button is the page's primary CTA, link and intent, labelled per plan; none on "No plan".
- Layout: the plans grid has one row per term; each plan spans them all with `grid-template-rows: subgrid`, so terms line up across plans. Pointing at a term lights it in every plan.
- Tokens: navy tiles remap `ink`, `ink-muted`, `line-strong` to on-navy values; limestone tiles a lighter limestone with `line-strong` hairline edges; numeral Geist 300 at up to 112 px; labels mono 12 px.
- Phones (pass 12, S11; under 860 px): the plans are a horizontal snap row of cards, `min(85%, 340px)` wide with the next one peeking; each card keeps its checker tiles (head with the numeral at 44 px inline beside the name, who it suits, the terms, the quote button at 48 px). Over the row, a strip of numeral tabs (48 px, numerals only, still navy/limestone; the plan name only in the `aria-label`) jumps to a plan and follows the swipe (`aria-current`); arrow keys move between tabs. Placeholder chips 13 px. svc-service.0: 1,126 -> 1,060 px at 375 (all four plans reachable by swipe, no names squeezed into tabs). The snap behaviour is `AQsnap` (the shared copy in `assets/src/parts/Snap.js` since round C; the look's own copy is deleted): a `[data-snap]` scroller, `[data-snap-to]` buttons in the same `[data-snap-box]`, `.is-snap` on the item in view and `aria-current` on its button; a button scrolls to its item (a jump with reduced motion). Snap rail (AQsnap): at the rail's end, where the last items cannot reach the start edge, the tapped item keeps the mark until the reader swipes.
- Don't use it for more than five plans or for comparisons that are not plans (CompareTable).

Motion: the checker lays itself down in a diagonal wave, head tiles first: each tile rises out of its bottom edge (clip-path, 0.7 s power3.inOut, 120 ms a step), its words follow 8 px, and the numerals roll on the odometer (sliding-number, `m.count`). Once, under 2 s. Term hover across plans repeats (class toggle, CSS transition).

Round C (pass 12): on phones every numeral tab is a limestone tile and the selected one (the plan in view) is the one navy tile; the strip is a `role="tablist"` of `role="tab"` buttons with `aria-selected`, `aria-controls` (the plan's `li` id) and a roving tab stop, mirrored from the snap rail's `aria-current` (look JS, a MutationObserver on `aria-current`). The 3 px flow outline is gone. Term lines (placeholders) 14 px mono on a 1.5 line box; labels 12 px. Desktop unchanged.
