Where water goes in reprocessing

  1. The last water on every instrument before drying. It needs critical water.

  2. Filled from the loop, at the grade your program sets for each step.

  3. Rinsing and flushing channels by hand, from taps on the loop.

  4. They filter their own feed further; the maker's instructions set what goes in.

  5. Utility water is enough here, not critical water.

  6. Feed water to the sterilizer maker's specification. We supply the water, not the steam.
