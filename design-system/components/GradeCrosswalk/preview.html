Which water each standard asks for

None of these is Purified Water. Purified Water is held to ≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25 °C (USP <645> stage 1), with no endotoxin limit.

  1. 01

    Critical water

    Fewer ions removed, far tighter on microbes, plus an endotoxin limit: < 10 µS/cm, < 10 colony-forming units (CFU)/mL, < 10 endotoxin units/mL

    • Hospital standardANSI/AAMI ST108:2023
    • CSA Z314:23
  2. 02

    Utility water

    Looser: < 500 µS/cm, < 500 CFU/mL. As the final rinse after high-level disinfection: < 10 CFU/mL and < 10 endotoxin units/mL

    • Hospital standardANSI/AAMI ST108:2023
  3. 03

    Type II reagent water

    Tighter on ions and organic carbon: < 1 µS/cm at 25 °C, total organic carbon < 50 µg/L

    values from D1193-06

    Type III reagent water

    Tighter on ions than Type II, looser on organic carbon: < 0.25 µS/cm at 25 °C, total organic carbon < 200 µg/L. Not a purity ladder

    Clinical laboratory reagent water
